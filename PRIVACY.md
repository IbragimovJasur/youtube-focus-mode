# Privacy Policy
YouTube Focus Mode – No Distractions  does not collect any data.

## What We Store
- Your settings (which features to hide)
- Stored locally on your device only

## What We Don't Collect
- Personal information
- Browsing history  
- Usage data
- Analytics

## Network Activity
None. No servers involved.

Available on GitHub: https://github.com/IbragimovJasur/youtube-focus-mode/
